Structural inspections

Cracks · Movement · Defects

Cracking, movement and distortion within a building can occur for many reasons and do not necessarily indicate a serious structural problem.

If concerns have been identified within a property, either by you, a building surveyor, mortgage lender or during building works, we can undertake a structural inspection to establish the likely cause and advise on what, if anything, needs to be done.

Our inspections focus on understanding why a building is behaving as it is, distinguishing between cosmetic defects and issues requiring further investigation or remedial work.

Following the inspection, we provide clear, practical advice and, where required, a written structural report setting out our observations, conclusions and recommendations.

Specific Structural Inspection (SSI)

A Specific Structural Inspection (SSI) is suitable where there is one clearly identified structural concern or localised area requiring investigation.

This might include a particular crack, bowing wall, movement around an opening, sagging floor or another isolated structural concern.

The inspection is limited to the area or issue agreed beforehand and provides a proportionate way of obtaining professional structural advice without commissioning an inspection of the entire property.

General Structural Inspection (GSI)

A General Structural Inspection (GSI) provides a broader visual assessment where there are several structural concerns or where the overall structural condition of the property needs to be considered.

Depending on the property and the concerns raised, we may assess cracking and movement internally and externally, walls and openings, floors, visible structural elements, previous alterations and other signs of structural distress.

We will consider the pattern and likely cause of any movement and advise whether the defects appear historic, ongoing or require further investigation.

Mortgage structural inspection

Mortgage Valuations · Lender Reports

If a mortgage valuation identifies cracking, movement or another potential structural concern, your lender may ask for a structural inspection before proceeding with the mortgage.

A Mortgage Structural Inspection (MSI) focuses specifically on the concerns raised by the mortgage valuer. The purpose of the inspection is to establish the likely cause and significance of the defect and provide clear recommendations where further investigation or remedial work is required.

Jack Olivia Structural Engineers can inspect the property and provide a clear written report addressing the structural concerns raised by your lender.

Following the inspection, we will explain our findings, identify any recommended next steps and provide a report that can be submitted to your lender or mortgage adviser.
