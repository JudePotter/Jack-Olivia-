Structural design

Steel · Timber · Masonry · Concrete

We provide structural design, calculations and drawings for residential and small-scale development projects, working with architects, homeowners and contractors from initial design through to construction.

Our designs consider not only structural performance, but also buildability, cost and how the structure integrates with the architecture.

Site visits & structural advice

Site Visits · Inspections · Practical Advice

Sometimes you don't need a full structural design — you simply need an engineer to look at something and give you clear advice.

A site visit can be used to discuss several structural matters during the appointment. This might include establishing whether a wall is load-bearing, looking at cracking or movement, discussing proposed alterations or providing advice on an issue discovered during construction.

Site visit — South East£295 + VAT
Site visit — London£395 + VAT

Includes up to one hour on site. Where the purpose of the visit is to establish whether a wall is load-bearing, we can follow up by email confirming our findings.

Where further calculations, drawings or a formal report are required, we will provide a clear fee before carrying out the additional work.

Structural inspections

Cracks · Movement · Defects

We inspect existing buildings where there are concerns about cracking, movement or other structural defects.

Following our inspection, we can provide a written structural report setting out our observations, likely causes and recommendations for any further investigation or remedial work.

Typical fee including site visit and written report£500–£650 + VAT

Loft conversions

Lofts · Roof Structures · New Floors

We provide complete structural packages for loft conversions, including the assessment of existing roof and floor structures and the design of new steelwork, floor joists, rafters and structural alterations.

Where possible, we look for efficient solutions that minimise unnecessary steelwork and disruption to the existing building.

Typical structural package£900–£1,250 + VAT

A look before you commit

Feasibility · Early Advice · Property Purchases

Before committing to a design, construction work or potentially even a property purchase, we can provide early structural advice to help identify what is feasible and where structural complexity or additional cost may arise.

For architects, this can also provide useful structural input during the early design stages, before layouts and architectural proposals become fixed.

On-site support

Builders · Site Queries · Construction

Construction does not always go exactly as shown on the drawings. Existing buildings can reveal unexpected structures and conditions once work begins.

We pride ourselves on being accessible and responsive when builders need structural input on site. Peter can be contacted directly to discuss time-sensitive structural queries, helping resolve issues quickly and keep construction moving.

Where a site visit is required, we will arrange one as quickly as reasonably possible.
