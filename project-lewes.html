Additional Floor, Lewes
Location Lewes
Type Extension & additional storey
Year 2025
Scope Additional storey · foundation assessment · localised underpinning · load-bearing wall removals · structural steelwork · large rear opening · side extension · alterations to existing structure

The complete transformation and extension of an existing mid-century home, including the addition of a new upper storey and extensive alterations to the existing structure.

The project involved the removal of internal load-bearing walls to create a more open plan arrangement, together with a substantial new opening to the rear of the property. New steelwork was designed to support the altered structure and facilitate the architectural layout.

A significant part of the engineering involved adding an entire additional storey over the existing building. The existing structure and foundations were assessed to establish their capacity to support the additional loads, with localised underpinning designed where required.

The works also included a new side extension and the structural integration of the new and existing elements of the property.

Working closely with the wider project team, the structural design allowed the existing building to be substantially reconfigured and enlarged while retaining and making efficient use of the original structure.
